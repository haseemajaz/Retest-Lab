Float quotient (short decimal):

Exact means arithmetic on the typed decimals, nearest tick with ties toward the higher price. The reference step mirrors floor(price / tick + 0.5 + 1e-10) using JavaScript double precision; the displayed quotient is a short decimal representation, not the exact binary fraction. Python then multiplies by its float tick and rounds the price to 10 decimal places. This view does not show that final price or determine which runtime is correct.

03 / Interpret

Context matters.

Differences can come from binary conversion, division or the reference’s fixed tolerance. This tool uses typed values, not the model’s upstream intermediate calculations. It does not identify a cause, adjust your data, test every intermediate result or recommend a tick. Check instrument rules and matched observations independently.

The inspector accepts up to 12 whole digits, 16 decimal places for price and 10 for tick. It is not the fixed-point trade-geometry calculator. Nothing is saved automatically.
