Entry-to-stop distance

    Each row shows configured R and effective R after tick rounding. Effective R is rounded to four decimals; “changed” means tick rounding altered the exact ratio. Half-tick price ties round toward the higher price.

    Inputs stay in this browser session. The CSV contains only this calculation; nothing is sent to a server by the calculator.

    Not investment advice or account-risk protection

    Entry-to-stop distance is a price difference, not a cash loss estimate. This tool does not model position size, fees, spread, slippage, gaps, partial exits or execution. Outputs are hypothetical and may differ from chart calculations.
