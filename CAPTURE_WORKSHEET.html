02 / Backup

Return to your work.

Save a private backup to resume later, including incomplete and unrecorded cells. Restoring replaces the worksheet only after confirmation when it contains cells.

Files stay on your device in the tested flow. Backups are not encrypted and can contain unchecked text; use trusted files and review restored cells. Nothing is saved automatically.

03 / Export

Keep the evidence together.

Download a single evidence bundle for the offline audit: it contains the checklist and recorded observations from one worksheet snapshot. Alternatively, download the CSVs separately without editing between downloads. Unrecorded cells appear only in the checklist; recorded blanks appear in both. Use a checklist with at least one cell, even if none have been recorded yet.

Run the audit with your prepared bars
python -m reference.audit_report --bars your_bars.csv --bundle retest_evidence_bundle.json --output your_audit.html
Use your actual filenames and a new output name. The bundle and bars are read separately.

Privacy: values stay in this page until you download them. Nothing is saved automatically; download a backup before refreshing or closing. Some browsers may warn before leaving. Unchecking Recorded retains its text in the worksheet but excludes it from export until rechecked. Downloads may contain sensitive information and are not encrypted. The bundle is for audit, not worksheet restore.

Spreadsheet warning: values are exported unchanged for the comparator. Text beginning with characters such as =, +, -, or @ may be interpreted as a formula by spreadsheet software. Treat CSVs as untrusted; use a plain-text editor or safe import settings.

The comparator trims surrounding whitespace and validates numeric fields; this worksheet checks structure, not the truth or numerical meaning of recorded values. See capture instructions and audit guide.
