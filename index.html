A simple framework

From breakout to retest.

Retest Lab makes its assumptions visible, so you can inspect the rules instead of relying on a black box.

01 / DETECT

Spot displacement

A confirmed close breaks a prior range with configurable candle-strength criteria. Optional EMA, relative-volume, VWAP and breakout-distance filters add context.

02 / WAIT

Watch the retest

The broken boundary becomes a modeled entry level. Setups expire; optional close confirmation and cancellation reasons clarify the observation.

03 / OBSERVE

Track the geometry

Four R-multiple levels, a stop and a status dashboard show the model’s state. Alerts fire on confirmed bars—not as orders.
