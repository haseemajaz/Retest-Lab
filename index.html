A simple framework

From breakout to retest.

Retest Lab makes its assumptions visible, so you can inspect the rules instead of relying on a black box.

01 / DETECT

Spot displacement

A confirmed close breaks a prior range with configurable candle-strength criteria. Optional EMA, relative-volume, VWAP and breakout-distance filters add context. The Pine indicator is unchanged from v0.3.

02 / WAIT

Watch the retest

The broken boundary becomes a modeled entry level. Setups expire; optional close confirmation and cancellation reasons clarify the observation.

03 / OBSERVE

Track the geometry

Four R-multiple levels, a stop and a status dashboard show the model’s state. View an ordered price ladder and compare up to three hypothetical setups; save or restore them locally, or use the offline trace, input preflight, guided comparison and optional observation checklist. Alerts are not orders.

VALIDATION RECORD

What has—and hasn’t—been checked

Checked: 162 Python reference/CLI tests and 70 calculator, comparison, file, undo and ladder tests passed on synthetic data. The prior v0.2 compiled on AAPL daily and had a five-bar replay smoke test; the unchanged v0.3 Pine script has not yet compiled or replayed.

Still required: Pine compile, replay and alert tests, cross-market paper observation and independent performance research. Account plan limits may prevent chart installation or saving.

Intrabar ambiguity: targets on the modeled fill bar are deliberately not recorded; genuine same-bar target hits can therefore be missed.
