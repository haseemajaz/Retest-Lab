A simple framework

From breakout to retest.

Retest Lab makes its assumptions visible, so you can inspect the rules instead of relying on a black box.

01 / DETECT

Spot displacement

A confirmed close breaks a prior range with configurable candle-strength criteria. Optional EMA and relative-volume filters add context.

02 / WAIT

Watch the retest

The broken boundary becomes a modeled entry level. Setups expire; a stop breach cancels a pending setup before a modeled fill.

03 / OBSERVE

Track the geometry

Four R-multiple levels, a stop and a status dashboard show the model’s state. Alerts fire on confirmed bars—not as orders.

VALIDATION RECORD

What has—and hasn’t—been checked

Checked: 35 synthetic Python tests passed. The script compiled on an AAPL daily chart and a five-bar TradingView replay showed no visible runtime error.

Still required: fuller replay coverage, alert testing, cross-market testing and independent evaluation of trading performance. Account plan limits may prevent saving.

Intrabar ambiguity: targets on the modeled fill bar are deliberately not recorded; genuine same-bar target hits can therefore be missed.
