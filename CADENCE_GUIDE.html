Replay data guide / v0.21.0

Look between the bars.

Consecutive indexes do not prove that the timestamps are evenly spaced. An optional preflight check helps you notice intervals that deserve review.

Advisory—not an exchange calendar.

Long intervals may be normal during closures, halts or session breaks. A clean check does not establish correct data, bar alignment or Pine parity.

01 / Interpret

What can the check tell you?

LONGER

Review the gap

An adjacent interval exceeds the value you chose. Check the original feed and exchange session before deciding whether anything is missing.

SHORTER

Review the boundary

A positive adjacent interval is shorter than expected. It may reflect the feed, bar convention, partial session or prepared data.

NOT CHECKED

Do not assume a pass

A blank or timezone-naive timestamp prevents that pair from being assessed. A single bar has no pair to compare.

REPEATED / REVERSED

Separate warnings

Equal or backward known instants are reported by existing timestamp-order checks, not counted as short intervals.

02 / Run locally

Choose an interval deliberately.

From the extracted project folder, use your prepared bar file and the expected duration in whole seconds. For example, 300 seconds means five minutes:

Preflight
python -m reference.preflight your_bars.csv --expected-seconds 300 --strict
Audit report
python -m reference.audit_report --bars your_bars.csv --expected-seconds 300 --output your_audit.html

Use your actual filenames and a new audit output name. Whole seconds from 1 to 31,536,000 are supported. Omit the option to leave the cadence check off. The input is never modified.

03 / Boundaries

Keep context alongside the result.

  • Only adjacent timestamps with explicit UTC offsets are compared as instants; missing or naive pairs are reported unchecked. No timezone is guessed.
  • Only positive intervals are categorized as short or long. Duplicate and backward instants use the existing ordering warnings.
  • Sessions, daylight-saving rules, expected bar count, data provenance and warm-up are not verified. Variable-length periods may not fit a fixed number of seconds.
  • Preflight exits 1 on warnings with --strict; without it, warnings can coexist with exit 0. Invalid input exits 2. Audit may also exit 1 for differences or missing observations.

The check is a diagnostic, not a market test, broker simulation or proof of correct signals. Consult replay capture instructions and validation record.
