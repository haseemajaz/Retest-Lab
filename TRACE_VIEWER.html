Offline research workspace / v0.24.0

Read the trace, bar by bar.

Open a reference-model trace CSV to explore its recorded states, events and levels. Your file stays on this device.

A trace is not a trading record.

This viewer does not run Pine, validate the file’s provenance or simulate orders. Counts reflect text in the file—not verified fills, returns or performance.

01 / Load

Choose a reference trace

Generate a trace with python -m reference.engine, then open its output CSV. Or start with a made-up example.

Maximum 2 MiB and 5,000 rows. Files are read locally and are not stored. Structural checks do not verify the content. See the replay field guide for preparation and limitations.
